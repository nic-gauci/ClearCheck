## Review procedure

Before enabling or materially changing a source, record its owner, exact endpoint, intended processing, update frequency, licence/permission, attribution, redistribution limits, personal-data implications, technical limits and approval owner. Save a dated copy or link to the applicable terms. Re-review at least annually and whenever the publisher changes the service.

Known upstream issue: legal designations can appear in the Official Journal before, or without, appearing in a consolidated machine-readable list. ClearCheck therefore requires both its current Commission snapshot and its recent-act Official Journal snapshot, records both versions, and never describes either dataset alone as exhaustive legal coverage. Named discrepancies are retained only as regression evidence, not as production exceptions.
