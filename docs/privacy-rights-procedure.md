# Privacy-rights procedure

Status: operational draft requiring approval by the final controller and Malta-qualified counsel.

1. Receive access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection requests through the authenticated account controls or the published privacy contact.
2. Record the requester, organisation, request type, received time and one-month response target. Do not place identity documents in ordinary support logs.
3. Verify identity proportionately. For organisation-controlled screening records, identify whether ClearCheck is controller or processor and notify the customer controller where required.
4. Locate account, membership, screening, report, billing and audit data. Restrict staff access to the case.
5. Assess applicable exemptions, AML/statutory retention, legal holds and legal-claims needs. A deletion request does not automatically delete regulated records.
6. Correct inaccurate account data promptly. For sanctions-source data, preserve the publisher record and help the requester contact the authoritative publisher; do not rewrite official source history.
7. Respond securely, explain the action or lawful reason for refusal/limitation, and explain complaint rights to Malta's Information and Data Protection Commissioner.
8. Record completion, evidence, approver and date. Escalate missed deadlines, disputes and suspected breaches immediately.

Never use a sanctions match alone to make a solely automated adverse decision. A trained reviewer must compare independent identifiers and document the decision.
